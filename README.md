# Threat Analysis, Risk Assessment & Incident Response Playbook (Bangladesh Bank Case Study)

This repository contains independent, OSINT-based cybersecurity threat analyses and operational frameworks modeled after the historic 2016 SWIFT cyber heist at Bangladesh Bank[cite: 4, 6, 17]. 

> **Academic & Professional Notice:** These documents are independent, illustrative risk assessments and incident response playbooks created solely for professional portfolio purposes. They are based entirely on publicly available open-source intelligence (OSINT), forensic reports, and media investigations[cite: 6].

## 📂 Repository Contents

### 1. Risk Assessment Report & Incident Response Playbook
- **File:** `Bangladesh Bank Risk Assessment Report and Incident Response Playbook.pdf`[cite: 2]
- **Highlights:** 
  - Asset-based risk matrix evaluating SWIFT terminals, operator credentials, verification printers, and logging systems[cite: 11, 12, 13].
  - Quantitative likelihood and impact scoring[cite: 9, 10].
  - Dual framework mapping linking vulnerabilities to **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)** and **MITRE ATT&CK** techniques[cite: 6, 25].
  - An 8-phase operational Incident Response Playbook featuring emergency SWIFT gateway isolation protocols and step-by-step flowchart logic[cite: 17, 18, 19, 20, 21].

### 2. OSINT Investigation & Phishing Simulation Exercise
- **File:** `OSINT on the Central Bank of Bangladesh.pdf`[cite: 28]
- **Highlights:**
  - Detailed reconnaissance case study analyzing public executive directories, LinkedIn structural patterns, forex milestone disclosures, and physical location exposure[cite: 30, 31, 32, 33].
  - A targeted spear-phishing simulation exercise demonstrating the weaponization of social engineering principles (authority, familiarity, urgency, and consensus)[cite: 35].
